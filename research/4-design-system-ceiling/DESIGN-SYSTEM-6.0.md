## Inputs and dropdowns

Heights present in the kit: 100px, 101px, 32px, 148px, 149px, 48px.
Default field height is 48px. Padding 12px on all sides. 1px border.
Resting border Obsidian/300 `#C6CED2`, field fill White/100.
The kit provides a label above the field, an optional hint below it, and an
error presentation that swaps the border and hint color to Electric Crimson.

## Tables

Heights present in the kit: 84px, 32px, 40px, 1316px, 10px, 12px.
Corner radii: 128px, 40px, 8px, 1000px.
Row height 48px at the default size, header row 40px.
Row divider 1px Obsidian/200 `#E6E8EA`.
Cell padding 12px horizontal.

## Tabs

Heights present in the kit: 56px, 48px, 40px, 29px, 23px, 20px.
Corner radii: 1000px, 16px, 12px, 8px.
Tab heights 40px, 48px and 56px depending on size. A small variant is 23px.
The selected tab is marked with a 2px Blue Spark/600 `#076EFF` underline.

## Badges

Heights present in the kit: 20px, 29px, 23px, 24px, 32px, 249px.
Corner radii: 1000px.
Pill shaped, radius 1000px. Heights 20px, 23px, 29px and 32px by size.
Padding 4px vertical, 12px horizontal.
Type maps to the color roles above, so Success uses Green Bell Pepper,
Danger uses Electric Crimson, and so on. The default badge is a tinted
background at step 100 or 200 with label text from the same family.

## Alerts

Heights present in the kit: 32px, 80px, 48px, 86px, 204px, 134px.
Corner radii: 16px, 12px.
Height 80px at the default size, radius 16px, padding 16px vertical and
32px left. Background is the role family at step 100.
Info alerts use Black/100 `#000000` background in the kit.

## Modals

Heights present in the kit: 32px, 48px, 200px, 312px, 249px, 120px.
Corner radii: 12px.
Radius 12px. Footer action buttons are 48px tall.
The kit shows a scrim behind the dialog.

## Tooltips

Heights present in the kit: 80px, 32px, 249px, 120px.
Corner radii: 12px.
Radius 12px. Dark surface with light text.

## Pagination

Heights present in the kit: 32px, 8px, 12px, 15px, 56px, 48px.
Corner radii: 8px, 16px, 12px.
The kit ships several pagination styles, including numbered controls and
compact dot indicators.

## Sliders

Heights present in the kit: 8px, 24px, 249px, 120px.
Corner radii: 1000px.
Track with a circular handle. Handle sits on a 48px tall control area.

## Toggles

Heights present in the kit: 24px, 48px, 249px, 120px.
Corner radii: 19px, 8px, 25px, 23px.
Switch track is 40x24px with a circular knob. Checkbox and radio controls
are 24x24px.

## Spacing and radius

Spacing steps used throughout the kit: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 80.
Corner radii used: 8, 12, 16, and 1000 for pill shapes.
Page gutter in the kit's own layouts is 80px at desktop width.
